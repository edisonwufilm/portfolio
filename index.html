About Me

Hello, I'm Edison Wu (WU Ka Ching). Currently pursuing a BFA in Film and Television (Year 1) at the Hong Kong Academy for Performing Arts (HKAPA), following my Higher Diploma in Creative Film Production (BUAF) at Hong Kong Baptist University.

Passionate about all aspects of on-set production, I specialize in Cinematography, Lighting, and Sound. With extensive practical experience across commercial productions, MVs, and films, I am most frequently active in the lighting department as an Electrician and Best Boy.

I approach every project and frame with 100% dedication and rigorous standards, constantly pushing myself to elevate the craft and deliver exceptional visual results.
